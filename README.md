# Creator Launch Alpha

**Start here:** click **Use this template** at the top of this page (or clone it), open the folder in Claude Code or Codex, and say hi. Or paste this into a fresh Claude Code or Codex session:

```text
Clone https://github.com/bilbop1/creator-launch-alpha.git into a new folder named my-channel, then work only inside that folder: read its AGENTS.md and CLAUDE.md, and run the setup wizard in .claude/skills/setup/SKILL.md exactly as written, starting with its two questions to me.
```

---

Make videos like the channel you wish you had. AI agents do the research, the scripts, the voice, the visuals and the editing. You make the taste calls.

It asks you two things and nothing else:

1. **What you have for it to edit.** Raw footage, you talking to camera, screen recordings, your voice, old videos, or nothing at all. Nothing is fine: then the agents make everything and nobody's face is in it.
2. **A link to a video or channel that looks like what you want yours to end up like.** More links are better.

From your link it works out the rest: Shorts, Reels or long videos, edited footage or 2D or 3D animation, the pacing, the voice and how often to post. It measures the top videos of that channel and its niche, studies how people are making this kind of video with AI agents right now, and tests every title, thumbnail, script and cut blind against the best competitor version until ours wins.

## What you need

Git, Python 3.10 or newer, and an AI coding agent (Claude Code or Codex). The agent installs the free helpers it needs, after asking.

Talk out loud if you can: use your mic or your phone's voice typing, and talk fast. Say what success looks like for you, even if it sounds huge or weird. Don't worry about how.

Nothing is posted, bought or shared without you saying so. It only posts when you say the word "post" for that exact finished video.
