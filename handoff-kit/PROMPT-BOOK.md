# The prompt book
Real messages from the person who built this system, sent to AI agents while making videos, tools and channels. They are raw. They swear, ramble and change their minds. That is the point: a user does not need polished prompts. They need to say what they want, what they hate, and how they will know it worked.
**How to use this book (agent).** Use these examples to model how to pull a vision out of the user: they say the whole idea, what good looks like, what they hate, and ask for proof. Match the situation they are in and copy the moves, not the words. Quote one short example to a stuck user only when it helps, and never point them at this file.
Each entry has the prompt, what it did, and why it works. Where the outcome could not be checked, it says so. Quotes are his words, with names of other people and any private details removed. A `[...]` means the rest of the message was cut for length. Words in square brackets replace a private name or fix a voice-typing slip.
Every entry comes from the two active channels, Market Tangle (finance-news long-form videos) and GeoGenius (map and news-on-the-map Shorts), or from the orchestrator that runs the work for both.

## Start a project by yapping

Get the user to say everything in their head first. Messy is fine. Include the weird goals and the thing they are afraid of. You can sort it out. You cannot read their mind.

## Set success criteria

Say what a good morning looks like, in a way you can check. Weird goals count. "Impress me" is a real goal if you also say what impressive means.

### 6. Short prompt, 2026-11-16, Market Tangle

> videos need to be 8 minutes though right? for midroll ads

**What it did:** The narration had come in at 7:47 and nobody had noticed. It became a hard rule: every Market Tangle video runs at least 8:00, the length is planned when the script is written, and a check fails anything shorter.

**Why it works:** One business fact (mid-roll ads need 8 minutes) becomes a number the agent can measure. Money goals are success criteria too.

### 7. Rule, 2026-11-16, Market Tangle

> i'm going to bed. when i wake up, the final will be ready. everything will be hill climbed and blind tested. i don't need to approve shit anymore against our competition. blind tested against our competition. hill climbed for parity but better via blind tests

**What it did:** It did not work the first time. He woke up to steps still waiting for his approval and was furious: "i told you to parity match everything via blind tests and not ask for my approval on shit." That led to the rule that no script reaches him until it has beaten the top competitors in a blind test.

**Why it works:** The finish line is checkable: the final is ready, it was blind tested, and no approvals are needed. When the agent missed it, he could point at his own words.

### 8. Short prompt, 2026-11-17, Market Tangle

> i'm going to bed. surprise me and impress me by the time i wake up. do not let this be a repeat of our last time i said this

**What it did:** This time the video was finished by morning. His first message: "honestly it looks ready to me. let's fucking post it." It went public that day.

**Why it works:** "Do not let this be a repeat" points at a known failure, so the agent checks for that failure first.

## Correct an agent

Name the exact thing that went wrong, say it should last forever, and say why. The best corrections here became rules the agent keeps.

### 9. Correction, 2026-11-14, GeoGenius

> i'm actually fucking pissed. why does every single geo short still come back with the text alignment on the left instead of centered in the video? not only the subtitles but cards and other text and shit i've literally made this note on every single iteration of every single video. that's calm dude. it's still fucking happening. hasn't been fucking fixed once. what the fuck is going on?

**What it did:** The agent finally found the cause. The layout code and every check both defined "centre" as the middle of a safe column, 60 pixels left of the real centre, so all the checks passed while every Short was wrong. A new check now measures the actual rendered pixels against the true centre, and he approved re-rendering all 11 Shorts.

**Why it works:** "Every single video" and "hasn't been fixed once" tell the agent the problem is in the system, not in one video. The lesson for any user: when a check keeps passing and you keep seeing the bug, the check has the same bug.

## Blind test against competitors

Your own checklist passing is not the bar. The bar is beating the best videos in the niche in a test where the judges cannot tell which one is yours. Scripts, thumbnails and titles all go through it: pairwise against top competitor videos, shown as feed cards, in both orders. A judge who knows your story is not blind, so a story-aware ranking is not a test. Each design improves on its own loop, using the judges' reasons.

### 21. Review note, 2026-11-11, Market Tangle

> this is not parity with our competitor. i want to see our script next to his. next time you show me ours

**What it did:** The agent built a side-by-side view: his lines with timestamps on the left, ours on the right, section by section. The first script had been 1,404 words against his 2,303. The next version was 2,193 against 2,303, with 8 of 8 chapters in his order. Every script card now shows this view.

**Why it works:** The agent had proved the script passed its own checks. He asked for the only comparison that matters: next to the winner.

### 22. Short prompt, 2026-11-17, Market Tangle

> that thumbnail sucks. does not beat our competition dude. you need to beat our competition via blind tests for the thumbnail

**What it did:** Thumbnails were judged blind against the top competitor thumbnails, in pairs and in both orders, by three different AI models. The best one scored 79 percent and beat 14 of 17. It lost only to one competitor video, and every judge named that video's title as the reason. That is how the title problem in entry 24 was found.

**Why it works:** "Sucks" is his taste. "Beat our competition via blind tests" turns his taste into a test the agent can run without him.

## Four habits that carry most of these

1. **Yap first.** Say the whole idea, then say: "Turn this into success criteria I can check, and tell me which ones are weak."
2. **Demand proof.** "Show me. A file I can open, a screenshot of the real result, or say not verified yet."
3. **Beat the best, blind.** "Before I see it, test it against the top competitor's best work with judges who can't tell which is ours. Improve each version from the judges' reasons."
4. **Turn every complaint into a rule.** "Make sure this never happens again, and write it down where every agent will see it."
