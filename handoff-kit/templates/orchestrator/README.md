# Your content orchestrator

One repo that manages all your channels. Each channel is its own project and repo. This one keeps the success criteria, the decisions waiting on you, and the method.

## What's here
- `AGENTS.md`: how your agent behaves (it will coach you, not flatter you).
- `handoff-kit/WORKFLOW.md`: how the whole thing works, in plain words.
- `handoff-kit/PROMPT-BOOK.md`: prompts that worked, to copy.
- `criteria/`: one success-criteria file per channel session.
- `DECK.md`: every decision waiting on you, one card each.
- `shared.md`: channels you run with other people.

## Use it
Open this folder in your agent (inside T3 Code) and type one of:
- `yap`: say everything on your mind. Weird and huge goals are welcome.
- `sweep`: get status on every channel in a few lines.
- `set me up`: add a channel or rebuild the layout.
