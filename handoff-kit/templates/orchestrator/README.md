# Your content orchestrator

This folder is the orchestrator. Each channel you launch lives in `channels/<name>/`. You need no channel, no idea and no accounts to start.

## Start
Open this folder in your agent and say hi (or "get me started"). It asks 5 easy questions, researches what already works with real proof, shows you 2 or 3 channel ideas, and launches the one you pick.

## What's here
- `AGENTS.md`: how your agent behaves (it coaches you, it doesn't flatter you).
- `channels/<name>/`: one folder per channel, with its research, criteria and plan for video 1.
- `criteria/`: one success-criteria file per channel.
- `DECK.md`: every decision waiting on you, one card each.
- `handoff-kit/WORKFLOW.md` and `PROMPT-BOOK.md`: how it works, and prompts that worked.
