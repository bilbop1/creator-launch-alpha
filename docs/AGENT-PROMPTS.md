# Stage prompts

Briefs for each stage, for you or a helper agent. Fill the brackets from the person's own words and the research. They are starting points; adapt them to the job. Every brief to a helper ends with "Please speed run this." and never carries a time estimate.

## Find the opportunity

> I want to build a channel for [audience]. Here are references: [URLs]. Find comparable channels and both successful and ordinary uploads. Explain the audience promise, observable creative grammar, selection limits, and three possible directions. Save evidence with links and dates in [project]. Ask me only for choices you cannot resolve.

## Decode the references

> For each reference I supplied, watch or inspect what your available tools actually allow. Break down the opening, premise, beats, visual storytelling, pacing, audio, title and ending. Mark anything you could not observe. Compare the video to its own channel baseline and look for replications and counterexamples. Propose testable hypotheses, not universal rules.

## Generate original concepts

> Based on the evidence in [project], make 12 distinct concepts. Consider universal stakes, public figures or creators where appropriate, and ideas that work without any famous person. For each, give a hook, escalation, visual payoff, audience, similar references and production difficulty. Compare variants where the creator is in the title, only the opening, or absent. Red-team overlap and rank the strongest three; don't reproduce a reference's script, character or visual design.

## Build the pilot

> Turn [selected concept] into a complete production brief, script, shot list, asset and sound plan, and editable pilot using the tools available here. Use my own or appropriately licensed assets. Choose an appropriate production method, show me any voice or look choices that need my taste, and check the full export for picture, sound, readability and story. Do not publish.

## Review a cut

> Review [file] as a viewer on a phone. Inspect the full video with audio. Identify the exact moments where the premise, action, joke or stakes are unclear; check captions, continuity, sound and ending. Distinguish measured faults from taste and report whether a new render was checked after fixes.

## Learn from a launch

> Compare [video] with the channel's own appropriately aged uploads and our predeclared target. Separate platform analytics from inference. Identify plausible explanations, limitations and the next test. Do not change metadata or post without my approval.

## Win the blind click test

> Run a strict blind click test for [titles / thumbnails / premise and opening] against the most-viewed similar competitor videos in [project]/research, using handoff-kit/BLIND-TEST.md and tools/feed_cards.py: mock phone feed cards, pairwise, both orders, no story context, judges mostly from other model families, never the maker. Test titles and thumbnails as a pair and also title-alone. Then climb each design on its own loop: read the judges' reasons, fix exactly that, retest, keep only if it scores higher. Report each design's score, pairs, families and whether it beats the best competitor card. Please speed run this.
